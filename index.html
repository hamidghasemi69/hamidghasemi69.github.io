About

I am a Principal Data Scientist in the Actionable Analytics group within the Office of the University Provost at Arizona State University, where I oversee the complete lifecycle of machine learning projects, from model development to deployment. Previously, I was a Senior Machine Learning Scientist at Overstock.

I earned my B.S. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Tehran, ranked first overall in Iran, and my M.S. and Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Iowa.
